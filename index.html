Privacy

Your screen stays yours.

  • Nothing leaves your Mac. No account, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Only when you ask. It captures one window, only when you press the hotkey.
  • Never saved. Screenshots live in memory and your clipboard — never on disk.
  • One network request. A daily update check you can switch off.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it send my message?

Never. ShowAI only pastes the image. You type your question and press Enter.

Which chat does the screenshot go to?

A chat you pinned in the menu; otherwise the only open chat, or the one you used last. The menu always shows “Sending to: …”.

Is it really free?

Yes. A Pro tier for AI agents and privacy controls is planned; the hotkey stays free.

What do the permissions do?

Screen Recording takes the screenshot. Accessibility presses ⌘V and brings the chat forward. Chrome control (optional) switches to your chat tab.

Safari, Arc, Firefox, Intel Macs?

Not yet — Google Chrome plus the ChatGPT and Claude apps, on Apple Silicon Macs.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu bar and delete it from Applications. Optionally run defaults delete app.showai.ShowAI.
