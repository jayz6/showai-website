Privacy policy

ShowAI sends nothing to us.

Last updated 15 November 2026 · applies to ShowAI 0.3 and this website

What ShowAI captures

Only when you press your ShowAI hotkey, ShowAI captures the window you're looking at, or the area you drag out. It never captures in the background and never records video.

Where your screenshot goes

Only into the AI chat you're using — the one shown in ShowAI's menu as "Sending to". From there, that service's own privacy policy applies (for example OpenAI, Anthropic or Google). ShowAI never sends your screenshot anywhere else, and never presses Enter for you.

What stays on your Mac

  • Screenshots exist only in memory and on your clipboard, marked so clipboard managers skip them. They are not saved to disk.
  • Settings — your hotkey, capture mode, pinned chat and added websites — are stored in macOS preferences on your Mac.
  • Nothing else is kept. No history of what you captured.

Network requests

ShowAI makes one kind of request: a daily check for updates, which downloads a small file from GitHub (github.com/jayz6/showai-releases). Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address. You can turn this off in ShowAI's menu (Automatically Check for Updates).

There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reports and no tracking in the app.

Permissions, and why

  • Screen Recording — to take the screenshot when you press the hotkey.
  • Accessibility — to press ⌘V for you and bring the right chat window forward.
  • Control Chrome (optional) — to find and switch to your AI chat tab.

You can revoke any of these in System Settings › Privacy & Security at any time.

This website

Hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs.
